## 4. 15-Minute Testing Protocol & Script

### Part 1: Context & Current Pain Points (3 minutes)
1. "How many hours per month do you currently spend categorizing messy bank feeds for your clients?"
2. "What are your biggest friction points with client communication when debit card purchases don't have receipts?"
3. "What concerns, if any, do you or your clients have regarding uploading financial records to cloud AI services?"

### Part 2: Hands-On Workflow Evaluation (8 minutes)
Invite the participant to interact with LedgerShield running the "Northstar Electrical Services" dataset:
1. **Import & Studio Scan:** "Take a look at the Reconciliation Studio table. Does the layout feel natural and readable compared to your usual tools?"
2. **Reconciliation Execution:** "Click 'Run Reconciliation'. Review the suggestions and confidence tags. Do the explanations make sense from an accounting perspective?"
3. **Inline Edit & Rule Learning:** "Change the category on a vendor line, and inspect the Transaction Drawer. Click 'Remember Rule'. Does this solve repetitive vendor matching for you?"
4. **Client Inquiry Builder:** "Open the Client Inquiry Builder. Look at the generated questions for Amazon and PayPal. Would you feel comfortable sending this markdown questionnaire directly to your client?"
5. **CSV Export:** "Click Export and review the pre-flight checks. Would this CSV fit into your import workflow for QuickBooks Online or Xero?"

### Part 3: Feedback & Commercial Validation (4 minutes)
1. "On a scale of 1–10, how well does this address your monthly reconciliation bottleneck?"
2. "What critical features are missing that would prevent you from using this in your daily practice?"
3. "If this tool were available as a standalone desktop app for $39 to $49 per month, would that be a reasonable business investment for your practice?"
